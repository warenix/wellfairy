綜援殮葬津貼（實報實銷至上限）

CSSA Burial Grant (actual costs to ERF-pegged cap)

綜援離世者：辦喪親友申領實際開支（死亡證、棺木、火化、禮堂）減帛金，以緊急救濟基金掛鈎上限為準（2026年4月起約$17,870）。憑死亡證＋單據向社署社會保障辦事處申請。

For deceased CSSA recipients: family/friend handling burial claims actual costs (certificate, coffin, cremation, hall) minus condolence money, capped at the ERF-pegged max (~$17,870 from Apr 2026). Apply at SWD Social Security Field Unit with death cert + receipts.
