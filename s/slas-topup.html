Supplementary Legal Aid (sandwich class, resources ≤$2.26m)

民事案資源超$452,320但≤$2,261,600。第一類（傷亡／僱員補償）：$1,000＋$113,080中期分擔。第二類（專業疏忽等）：$5,000＋10%或$113,080（取高）。勝訴扣20%（審前和解15%）。

For civil cases where resources exceed OLAS ($452,320) but ≤$2,261,600. Type I (injury/death/compensation): $1,000 fee + $113,080 interim. Type II (professional negligence etc.): $5,000 fee + 10% or $113,080 (higher). Success deduction 20% (15% pre-trial).
