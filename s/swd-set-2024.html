殘疾人士就業展能（綜合就業培訓，2024年4月起）

Supported Employment & Training for Disabled (SET, from Apr 2024)

綜合就業展能＋在職培訓＋啟航：15歲+殘疾（青年線15–25歲含早期思覺徵兆）享輔導、180小時培訓、≤3個月實習、≤6個月試工（僱主工資補助≤$4,000／月）、12個月跟進。經14–26間NGO。僱主：購置輔助器材／改裝高達$40k。
Integrated SE + OJT + Sunnyway: 15+ disabled (youth stream 15–25 incl. early psychosis signs) get counselling, 180-hr training, ≤3-mo attachment, ≤6-mo trial with employer wage subsidy ≤$4,000/mo, 12-mo post-placement. Via 14–26 NGOs. Employers: SPED up to $40k devices/workplace mods.
