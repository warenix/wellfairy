普通科門診公私營協作計劃（病情穩定病人：每年10次資助私家診症）

HA GOPC PPP (stable chronic patients: 10 subsidised private visits/yr)

病情穩定的醫管局門診病人獲邀轉看自選私家醫生：每年最多10次資助診症（慢性＋偶發疾病，診所即時配藥，轉介化驗/X光），收費與醫管局家庭醫學診所相同：$150/次＋每種計劃藥物$5（4星期）；基礎化驗免費。只限獲邀參加；計劃預計2028年結束並過渡至慢病共治計劃。
Stable HA clinic patients invited to see a chosen private doctor: up to 10 subsidised visits/yr (chronic + episodic care, drugs dispensed at the clinic, referred labs/X-ray). Pay HA FMC rates: $150/visit + $5 per programme drug item (4 weeks); basic labs free. Invitation only; ends 2028 with transition to CDCC.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

點解符合？Why you may qualify

Already stable at an HA clinic and got the letter — same $150 fee, private doctor near home.
