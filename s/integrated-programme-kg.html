Integrated Programme in Kindergarten-cum-Child Care Centre (mild disabilities, ages 2 to under 6)

讓2至6歲以下輕度殘疾兒童在普通幼稚園暨幼兒中心接受訓練及照顧，融入主流學校和社會。轉介免費；除幼稚園費用自付外不另收費。
Training and care for children aged 2 to under 6 with mild disabilities inside an ordinary kindergarten-cum-child care centre, easing integration into mainstream school and society. Referral is free; no extra charge apart from the kindergarten fees you pay.
