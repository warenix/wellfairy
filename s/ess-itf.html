Enterprise Support Scheme (ESS)

每項項目最高1000萬港元的美元對配資助，用於院內研發；最長項目時長24個月
Dollar-for-dollar matching up to $10 million per project for in-house R&D; max 24 months project duration

截止日期 Deadline：Rolling (applications all year round)

點解符合？Why you may qualify

您的公司在香港註冊成立，有效營業登記證，並從事院內研發工作；您可能有資格申請最高1000萬港元的美元對配資助。

Your company is incorporated in Hong Kong, has valid business registration, and is conducting in-house R&D work; you may apply for dollar-for-dollar matching funding up to HK million.
