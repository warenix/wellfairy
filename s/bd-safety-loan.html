Building Safety Loan (up to $1m/unit, interest-free path)

私人樓宇業主：每單位高達$100萬做維修、消防、升降機、渠務、僭建、斜坡。付息貸款免審查；綜援／長生津／低收入免息（60歲+單人入息≤$13,455、資產≤$415k）。60歲+困難可延至轉售／身故還。

Private-building owners: loans up to $1m/unit for repairs, fire safety, lifts, drainage, UBW removal, slopes. No means test for interest-bearing; interest-free for CSSA/OALA/low-income (60+ single income ≤$13,455, assets ≤$415k). 60+ hardship may defer to sale/death.
