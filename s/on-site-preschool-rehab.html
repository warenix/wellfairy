到校學前康復服務（免費，在就讀幼稚園接受）

On-site Pre-school Rehabilitation Services (free, in your kindergarten)

為6歲以下輕度殘疾或懷疑有特殊需要兒童，在其就讀幼稚園內提供免費到校康復訓練（跨專業團隊：職業／物理／言語治療、心理、社工）。逾10,000個名額；同時支援老師及家長。
Free on-site rehab training and support for children under 6 with mild disabilities or suspected special needs, delivered in their own kindergarten by inter-disciplinary teams (OT, physio, speech, psychology, social work). Over 10,000 places; teachers and parents coached too.
