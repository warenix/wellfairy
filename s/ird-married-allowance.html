Salaries Tax: Married Allowance ($290,000 from 2026/27)

2026/27起已婚納稅人減$290,000（之前$264k）。年內任何時間已婚、分開報稅除外；配偶無入息或合併／個人入息課稅。

$290,000 off for married taxpayers from 2026/27 ($264k before). Married any time in year, not living apart; spouse no income or joint/personal assessment.
