醫管局體外授精名額增至每年1,900個 (2028-29)

HA IVF Quota Increase (to 1,900/yr by 2028-29)

三年逐步由1,600(2026-27)增至1,900個體外授精周期/年(共+300)。瑪麗醫院2027年首季增100。適用於不育夫婦/癌症患者。瑪麗/威爾斯親王/廣華醫院提供公營資助服務。
Progressive increase from 1,600 (2026-27) to 1,900 IVF cycles/yr over 3 years (+300 total). QMH +100 by 1Q27. For infertile couples/cancer patients. Subsidised public service at QMH, PWH, KWH.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

Couple needing IVF — HA adding 300 more cycles over 3 years, wait time cut from 24-36 to 12-24 weeks.
