Aged 55+ with HKID: pledge a paid-up HK-authorised life policy (non-investment-linked, death benefits ≤$15m aggregate) for monthly payouts over 5/10/15/20 yrs or life, plus lump sums. No lifetime repayment; shortfall insured by HKMCI.
