直資學校學費減免（不遜於政府資助標準）

DSS School Fee Remission (≥ govt SFAA benchmarks)

每間直資學校必須設不遜於政府學資處標準的學費減免／獎學金，撥出≥10%學費收入。綜援／學資處家庭原則上獲減免 — 開學前經學校申請。
Every DSS school must run fee remission/scholarship at least as generous as govt SFAA schemes, setting aside ≥10% of fee income. CSSA/SFAA families in principle offered remission — apply via the school before term.
