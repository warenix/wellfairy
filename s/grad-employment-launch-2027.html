畢業生就業啟航計劃 (2027–28)

Graduate Employment Launch (2027–28)

僱主資助：每月薪金三分一，上限$6,000/月，資助期6個月。2027年首季起兩年增設10,000全職職位。香港居民，29歲或以下，全職經驗≤6個月，非全職受僱。
Employer subsidy: 1/3 of monthly salary up to $6,000/month for 6 months per graduate. 10,000 full-time posts over 2 years from Q1 2027. For HK residents ≤29 with ≤6 months full-time experience, not in full-time employment.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

剛畢業經驗少 — 僱主有補貼願意聘用你。

Fresh graduates with little experience — employer gets subsidy to hire you.
