Kindergarten Education Scheme (half-day free)

計劃幼稚園（非牟利、本地課程）：約九成半日制免費；全日制低收費。K1憑註冊證／入學許可入學。有需要家庭另加KCFRS學費減免。
Scheme-KGs (NPM, local curriculum): ~90% of half-day places free; whole-day kept low-fee. Enrol K1 with RC/AP. Needy families add KCFRS fee remission on top.
