Old Age Living Allowance $4,345/mo

每月$4,345。單人入息≤$10,900資產≤$415,000；夫婦≤$16,680/≤$630,000。不可同領高齡/傷殘/綜援。
$4,345/mo. Single: income ≤$10,900 + assets ≤$415,000. Couple: ≤$16,680 + ≤$630,000. Not with OAA/DA/CSSA.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

You are 65+ within income/asset limits.
