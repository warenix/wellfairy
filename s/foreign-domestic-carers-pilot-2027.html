外籍家庭照顧者先導計劃 (2027下半年, 每年數百名額)

Foreign Domestic Carers Pilot (H2 2027, few hundred/yr)

2027下半年試行：輸入外籍家庭照顧者，需≥3年長者照顧經驗+150小時認可培訓(原居地)，經港府委託機構評核合格。最低工資比外傭最低工資高10-20%。首年數百名額。現職外傭須返鄉受訓。詳情2027年中公布。
Pilot H2 2027: admit foreign domestic carers (FDCs) with ≥3yr elderly care experience + 150hr accredited training (origin country), assessed by HK-commissioned body. Min wage 10-20% > FDH MAW. Initial few hundred/yr. FDHs must return to origin for training. Details mid-2027.
