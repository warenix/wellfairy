Innovation & Technology Scholarship (up to $150,000 + overseas attachment)

非應屆畢業本地本科生（任何學科）高達$150,000：海外／內地交流（≥3週，須含內地）、師友、服務計劃、本地科企實習。每年25名，經大學提名（第16屆，累計400人）。
Up to $150,000 for non-final-year local undergrads (any discipline): overseas/Mainland attachments (≥3 weeks, incl. Mainland leg), mentorship, service project, local tech internship. 25 awards/yr via university nomination (16th year, 400 alumni).
