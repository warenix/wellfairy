Day Care Centre for Elderly

為經安老服務統一評估機制評估的60歲或以上體弱長者提供日間照顧、復康運動及社交活動，協助居家安老；亦支援照顧者
Centre-based day care, rehabilitation exercises and social activities for frail elderly aged 60+ assessed under the Standardised Care Need Assessment Mechanism, to age in place; carer support included
