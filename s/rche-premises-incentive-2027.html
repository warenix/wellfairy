安老院舍 premises 激勵優化 (房屋局/發展局, 2027年起)

RCHE Premises Incentive Enhancement (HB/DEVB, from 2027)

容許發展商在新私人發展項目中,利用豁免計算總樓面面積的樓面面積,提供長者日間照顧設施。激勵提供安老院舍/日間照顧。
Allow developers to use GFA-exempt floor area for elderly day-care facilities in new private developments. Incentivises RCHE/day-care provision.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

點解符合？Why you may qualify

發展商 — 新樓宇加入長者日間照顧設施可獲豁免總樓面面積。

Developer — GFA exemption for including elderly day-care in new buildings.
