Stamp Duty Waiver for Newborn Families (up to $20,000, from 2027)

購買住宅物業由子女出生前1年至出生後2年(子女須於2026年9月16日或後在港出生)，可獲印花稅從價稅寬免上限$20,000。父或母需為香港永久居民。法案2027上半年提交立法會。尚未生效。
Up to $20,000 AVD waiver for residential property purchased 1 year before to 2 years after birth of child born ≥16 Sep 2026 in HK. Either parent must be HKPR. Bill to LegCo H1 2027. Not yet in effect.
