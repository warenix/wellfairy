Neighbourhood Child Care (fee-waivable, 7am-11pm)

輪班／無支援家長的新生至小學生彈性照顧（朝7晚11），18區都有。家長付上門每小時$28、中心每小時$20；困難可申請費用減免（免費）。亦可做社區褓姆賺每小時$40-$60（照顧0-3歲／特殊學習需要每小時$60，3-9歲每小時$40）。
Flexible 7am-11pm care for newborn-to-primary kids of shift/unsupported parents, all 18 districts. Parents pay $28/hr home-based or $20/hr centre-based; fee waiver for hardship (free). Or earn $40-$60/hr as a community carer ($60 for 0-3s/SEN, $40 for ages 3-9).
