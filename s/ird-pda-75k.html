Salaries Tax: Personal Disability Allowance ($75,000, self)

本人符合傷殘津貼（社署認證重度殘疾）：薪俸稅每年減$75,000，受養人免稅額以外另計。每年BIR60申索。

If you yourself are DA-eligible (certified severely disabled, SWD): $75,000/yr off your salaries tax on top of dependant allowances. Claim in BIR60 yearly.
