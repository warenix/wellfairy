房委會樂齡科技先導計劃 (長者住戶, 2027-28起)

HKHA Gerontechnology Pilot (Elderly Households, from 2027-28)

為高齡獨居長者及全長者戶引進家用樂齡科技設備，於高長者比例公屋邨試行。首階段(2027-28)5個屋邨、約1,500戶。設備類型(如跌倒偵測)待定。逐步推展至其他屋邨。
Household gerontech equipment for elderly singletons & all-elderly households in selected PRH estates with high elderly population. 5 estates, ~1,500 households first phase (2027-28). Equipment type (e.g. fall detection) TBD. Gradual extension to other estates.
