兒童發展基金

Child Development Fund

10-14歲低收入家庭兒童的儲蓄匹配賬戶和導師計劃。儲蓄最高每年$10,000的1:1或1:2匹配，生活技能訓練，目標設定和職業體驗。經社署社工或NGO申請。

Savings-matched accounts and mentoring for children aged 10-14 from low-income families. 1:1 or 1:2 matching on savings up to $10,000/yr, life skills training, goal setting and career exposure. Apply via SWD social workers or NGOs.
