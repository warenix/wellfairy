為拯救他人而犧牲生命者家屬的經濟援助計劃 $600萬-$1,200萬

Financial Assistance Scheme for Family Members of Those Who Sacrifice Their Lives to Save Others $6M-$12M

一次性資助 $600萬至$1,200萬，依年齡、正常退休年齡及現行每月就業收入中位數計算。為嘗試拯救或保護他人而犧牲生命者之家屬。

One-off grant $6M-$12M based on age, normal retirement age and prevailing median monthly employment earnings. For families of persons who lost their lives attempting to save or protect others.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

點解符合？Why you may qualify

你是為拯救或保護他人而犧牲生命者的家屬。

You are family of someone who died saving or protecting others.

請帶齊文件 Documents to bring

  • 死亡證明書
  • 與死者關係證明
  • 警方報告/事件證據
  • 已填妥申請表
