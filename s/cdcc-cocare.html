Chronic Disease Co-Care (45+, subsidised family doctor)

45歲+無已知糖尿病/高血壓：資助篩查、覆診（每年最多4–6次）、化驗、基本藥物，配對家庭醫生。65歲+可用醫療券付自付額。

45+ with no known diabetes/hypertension: subsidised screening, consultations (up to 4–6/yr), labs, basic drugs via paired family doctor. 65+ may pay copay with HCV.
