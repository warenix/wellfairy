Kindergarten-cum-Child Care Centre Day Care (birth to under 3)

初生至3歲以下日間照顧（獨立幼兒中心及幼稚園附設中心），另有暫託及延長時間服務。幼兒中心家長津貼免審查；低收入家庭可經在職家庭及學生資助事務處申請學費減免，資助部分或全部費用。
Day care for children from birth to under 3 at standalone centres and centres attached to kindergartens, plus occasional and extended-hours sessions. Child Care Centre Parent Subsidy needs no means test; low-income families can get fee remission covering part or the whole fee via WFSFAA.
