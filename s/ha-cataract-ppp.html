白內障手術計劃（$8,000資助＋自付≤$8,000）

Cataract Surgeries Programme ($8k subsidy + ≤$8k copay)

在醫管局白內障例行輪候名單（穩定組、可局部麻醉）：獲邀私家手術 — 資助$8,000、自付上限$8,000；合資格豁免（綜援／全免／75歲+長生津／長者券第0級）經名單醫生$0。須登記醫健通。
On HA routine cataract waitlist (routine group, local anaesthesia): invitation to private surgery — $8,000 HA subsidy, copay capped $8,000; waiver-eligible (CSSA/full-waiver/OALA75+/RCSV-L0) pay $0 with listed doctors. eHealth registration required.
