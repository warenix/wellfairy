社區藥房計劃（就近取可負擔基層醫療藥物）

Community Pharmacy Programme (affordable primary-care drugs nearby)

2026年第四季起：在社區藥房以低於市價取社區藥物名冊基層醫療藥物（推出時100多項，逐步增至200-400項，醫管局統籌採購）。醫管局家庭醫學門診病人下次覆診前分期取藥；慢病共治等計劃參加者可憑醫生處方選擇在社區藥房取藥及接受用藥輔導。院舍模式2027年第一季起（配藥、整合、預先包裝每日藥量＋藥劑師藥物安全支援）。首階段4區x4-5間藥房，2027年第四季擴至全港18區。
From Q4 2026: collect Community Drug Formulary primary-care drugs (100+ items at launch, growing to 200-400) at community pharmacies below market via HA joint procurement. HA Family Medicine outpatients get batch refills before next follow-up; CDCC-type programme enrollees can use a pharmacy instead of the family-doctor clinic with a prescription, plus counselling. Residential-care-home arm from Q1 2027 (dispensing, reconciling, prepacked daily doses + pharmacist drug-safety support). First phase 4 districts x 4-5 pharmacies, all 18 districts by Q4 2027.
