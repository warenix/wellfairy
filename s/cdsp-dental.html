社區牙科支援計劃 (任何年齡)

Community Dental Support (CDSP, any age)

取代關愛基金長者牙科服務資助(2026年1月停)。資助洗牙補牙杜牙根等。75歲+長者生活津貼/一級家居照顧/全免醫療費用者免行政費。熱線2111 3403。

Replaces CCF dental (ended Jan 2026). Subsidised checkup/scale/extract/fill/X-ray/root canal. Admin fee waived for 75+ OALA, Level-1 home care, full-waiver holders. Hotline 2111 3403.

截止日期：長期有效 Ongoing

You hold OALA or a fee waiver — dental covered.
